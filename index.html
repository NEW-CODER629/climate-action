🌳 How tree verification works

  1. Join or log in to your account.
  2. Plant a tree and record a short video of the activity.
  3. Submit the video and basic planting details.
  4. A reviewer checks the submission.
  5. Only approved submissions add to the verified tree total.
Please get permission before recording other people. Avoid showing your home address, school details, phone number, or other private information in a video.
